# Outreach: small Milan galleries (free listing)

**Audience:** gallery managers / press contacts of small and mid-size Milan galleries (start with venues already in our data: 29 venues live, see R11 coverage list when it exists).
**Message:** we already list your current show for free, from your public site. Check it, tell us if anything is wrong, and link to it if you like. No fee, no account.
**Where it goes:** the CEO sends from the company address, one email per gallery, personalised line filled in by hand. Send from ~10 Dec (needs the public URL, R3). Do not send before the link works.
**Success signal (analyst):** replies count; visits to `/e/:id` with a referrer or `?ref=gallery` from galleries; number of corrections received. Target: 5 replies and 3 galleries linking by 31 Jan.
**Prerequisite:** R8 (per-show URLs). Pick a real show for each email and check the listing is correct first. Never claim visitor numbers; we have none yet.
